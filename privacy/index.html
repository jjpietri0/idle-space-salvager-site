Privacy Policy: Idle Space Salvager

Effective 28 October 2026

Idle Space Salvager is made by Jurica Pietri, Croatia ("we"). You can reach us at appleinteractivegames@gmail.com.

In short

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.
  • Four services receive data from the game: PostHog (anonymous usage analytics, only if you agree), Sentry (crash reports), Google AdMob (ads you choose to watch) and RevenueCat (to record purchases). Apple and Google handle all payments.

On your device

Your save game and settings are stored only on your device. We don't receive them, and they're deleted when you uninstall the game.

Usage analytics (PostHog)

To learn where players get stuck and how to balance the game, the game sends usage events to PostHog, on servers in the EU:

  • a random id created when the game is installed, not linked to you;
  • what happens in the game: opening it, tutorial steps, founding a corporation, charting a sector, jumping to a galaxy, watching an ad, buying something, and which screens you open;
  • device and app details: device model, operating system and version, app version, language and screen size.

PostHog also receives your IP address when the game sends an event; we have set PostHog to discard it rather than store it.

The game asks the first time you open it and sends nothing unless you agree. You can change your answer at any time in Settings, under Usage analytics. We keep analytics data for up to 24 months.

Crash reports (Sentry)

When the game crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Sentry so we can fix it: what went wrong and where in the game's code, the device model, operating system and version, app version, and the steps in the game just before the error. Reports carry no names, emails or other personal details. We use Sentry's EU data region and have set it not to store IP addresses. We rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working, and keep reports for up to 90 days.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game sets reminders on your device when you leave it: when your fleet's time away is full, and when the daily supply drop is ready. They're scheduled by your phone itself; nothing about them is sent to us. You can turn them off in Settings, under Reminders, or in your phone's settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows an ad only when you choose to watch one for a reward. Google AdMob, run by Google, provides these ads. To show and measure them, Google may collect your device's advertising id, IP address, device details and how you interact with the ad, and, with your consent, use them to personalize ads.

On iPhone, the game also asks once whether ads may use your device's advertising identifier (Apple's tracking prompt). If you allow it, Google may use the identifier to personalize ads and measure them across apps and websites; if you don't, ads still appear but aren't personalized. You can change this at any time in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad loads. You can change it at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices. Google's use of data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat)

Purchases are paid through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat checks and records purchases for us, so that what you buy is delivered and can be restored. It receives the store's purchase receipt, a random user id and device and app details. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

Transfers outside the EU

Google, RevenueCat and Sentry are based in the United States. Data sent to them may be processed there, under the safeguards EU law requires, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Turning off Usage analytics stops all analytics at once. Because the data isn't linked to your name, we may ask for details such as your device model and when you installed the game to find it. You can also complain to the Croatian data protection authority (AZOP, https://azop.hr) or the authority where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the game if the change is significant.
