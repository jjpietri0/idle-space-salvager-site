Support: Idle Space Salvager

Something wrong, or an idea? Email us at appleinteractivegames@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, its iOS version and what happened, and we'll answer as soon as we can.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. There are no accounts, so nothing is stored online. Deleting the game deletes your progress with it.

I bought something and didn't get it

Open Settings (the gear at the top right) and tap Restore purchases. That brings back one-time items such as the Starter pack, Double income and Long shift. Gems, boosts and income packs are paid out the moment you buy them. If something is still missing, email us with the order ID from your receipt from Apple.

Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I turn off reminders?

In the game's Settings, switch off Reminders. You can also turn off notifications for the game in your iPhone's Settings.

Ads and privacy

Ads only play when you choose to watch one for a reward. Whether they're personalized follows your answer to Apple's tracking question; you can change it in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game's Settings also have Ad privacy choices, under Privacy.

Saw an ad that shouldn't have been there? Tap Report an ad in the game's Settings, or email us what it was and when you saw it.

Anonymous usage data is only sent if you agreed to it, and you can switch it off at any time in Settings → Privacy, under Usage analytics. The full details are in our privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting the game removes everything stored on your phone. To ask us to delete usage data or crash reports, email appleinteractivegames@gmail.com.
